Reclaim: Earn Your Screen Time

Privacy Policy

Reclaim is built to help you trade intentional activity for screen time. This policy explains what the app handles, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

Effective and last updated: July 27, 2026

The short version: Reclaim does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. Raw Screen Time activity and raw HealthKit samples stay on your device. A limited set of account and recovery data is stored in Firebase so your account can work and eligible progress can be recovered.

Information Reclaim collects

When you create or use a Reclaim account, the app may process:

  • Account information: your Firebase user ID, email address, optional display name, sign-in provider, and account creation and update dates.
  • App progress and settings: wallet balance, wallet cap, earned and spent minutes, intentional-pass totals, exchange-rule settings, Momentum status, and daily or weekly progress summaries.
  • Regional information: your device's time-zone identifier, used to place progress into the correct local day and week. Reclaim does not request or upload GPS coordinates.
  • Limited recovery evidence: short-lived, hashed receipt identifiers; a broad source such as walking, workout, Lichess, or intentional pass; the minute adjustment; and its date. This helps restore earned time without awarding it twice. Qualified Momentum day keys may also be stored for streak recovery.

Information that stays on your device

Reclaim is designed so the most sensitive underlying activity is processed locally:

  • Screen Time: selected apps, websites, and categories; opaque Screen Time tokens; and raw usage reports remain inside Apple's privacy-preserving Screen Time system on your device.
  • Health and fitness: raw HealthKit samples, step counts, workout details, routes, heart rate, and calories are not uploaded by Reclaim. Reclaim requests read-only access and converts eligible activity into reward minutes on-device.
  • Lichess: your OAuth token is kept in the iOS Keychain. Detailed puzzle data and activity are processed locally. If a Lichess activity creates a reward, only the limited recovery evidence described above may be synced to Firebase.
  • Local ledger details: original provider identifiers and detailed wallet descriptions are not uploaded.

How information is used

Reclaim uses information only to provide app functionality, including to:

  • authenticate your account and keep it secure;
  • calculate, display, sync, and recover your wallet and Momentum progress;
  • prevent the same activity from being rewarded more than once;
  • connect optional services you choose to enable; and
  • respond to support, privacy, and account-deletion requests.

Services Reclaim relies on

Reclaim shares limited information with service providers only when needed to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication manages sign-in, and Cloud Firestore stores account, summary, and recovery data. Data is associated with your Firebase user ID. Learn more in Firebase's privacy information.
  • Apple: Reclaim uses Apple services such as Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, Screen Time APIs, and TestFlight. Apple's handling of information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Lichess: if you choose to connect Lichess, Reclaim communicates directly with Lichess to access account and puzzle activity using the permission you grant. See the Lichess Privacy Policy.

Reclaim does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics services.

Tracking, advertising, and sale of data

Reclaim does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, does not serve targeted advertising, and does not sell or rent personal information.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account, summary, recovery, and Momentum data is retained while your Reclaim account is active. The detailed recovery-receipt window is limited to approximately eight days. Local data remains on your device until you delete the account, reset the app, or remove the app, subject to iOS behavior.

You can delete your Reclaim account in the app from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This deletes Reclaim's Firebase account data and authentication account after any required recent sign-in. If you cannot access the app, contact ejosterman@gmail.com. Deleting Reclaim does not delete accounts or information held independently by Apple, Lichess, or another service.

Service providers may retain limited security, fraud-prevention, or operational records as required by law or under their own policies.

Security

Reclaim uses platform protections including authenticated, user-scoped Firebase access, Apple's Keychain for the Lichess token, and Apple's privacy-preserving Screen Time framework. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but the app limits what it sends off-device and uses reasonable safeguards for the information it handles.

Children's privacy

Reclaim is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the developer so it can be addressed.

International processing

Reclaim's service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Reclaim's features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page will identify the current version. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel when required.

Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, email ejosterman@gmail.com. For app help, visit the Reclaim Support page.
