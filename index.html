What stays on this iPhone

Time, progress & settings

Reclaim stores your time balance, earning and spending records, streak, focus sessions, daily targets, reward settings, and connection sync records locally. The current app does not upload your balance or streak to Firebase and does not offer cloud wallet recovery.

Apple Health

With your permission, Reclaim reads steps, flights climbed, and workouts to verify movement rewards. It processes Health samples and reward evidence locally and does not upload raw HealthKit data to Reclaim’s servers. Reclaim does not write data to Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising or sold.

Screen Time

Protected-app, category, and website selections, opaque Screen Time tokens, and usage reports are handled on this iPhone using Apple’s Screen Time frameworks. Reclaim does not upload those selections or raw reports. Local time enforcement can continue without an internet connection.

Before you reinstall: removing Reclaim or moving to another iPhone can erase local history. Signing in again does not restore your wallet or streak from Reclaim’s cloud.

Optional connected services

You choose whether to connect these sources. Each provider receives the authorization and API requests needed for its feature and handles its own account information under its privacy policy.

Lichess

Reclaim communicates directly with Lichess to read account and puzzle activity. Your access token is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain. Connected-account and puzzle-completion records are kept locally for reward verification and duplicate prevention; Reclaim does not upload them to Firebase or retain puzzle positions or solutions as cloud records.

Strava

Reclaim’s server stores encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens, your Strava account ID, granted scope, and connection timestamps. It temporarily processes activity responses and sends the app activity IDs, type, start time, moving duration, distance, and manual/flagged/deleted indicators to verify rewards. Reclaim does not save workout details in its cloud database. Pending webhook records contain activity IDs and timestamps, with a seven-day expiry. Routes, heart rate, photos, and descriptions are not retained or sent to the app for reward verification.

Todoist

Reclaim’s server stores encrypted OAuth access and refresh tokens, your Todoist account ID, connection timestamps, and your selected task scope or project ID. It reads completed-task responses, then sends the app task IDs, project IDs, and creation/completion times. Project names are passed to the app so you can choose a project. Reclaim does not retain task titles or notes in its cloud database or send them to the app for reward verification.

Account, configuration & technical data

Sign in with Apple

Firebase Authentication manages your Reclaim account. It processes your account ID, sign-in-provider information, authentication timestamps, and the name or email Apple provides, including a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email. The current app offers Apple sign-in; older accounts may contain information supplied through earlier sign-in methods.

Earning configuration

Firebase Remote Config delivers earning rules and caps. It processes app and device information such as country, language, time zone, app identifiers, operating-system version, and SDK version. Its supporting installation service uses an app-installation identifier. Reclaim does not request GPS permission for this feature.

Diagnostics & support

Firebase SDKs process limited technical metadata about the app, device, and SDK environment. Our backend and hosting providers may process request information, including IP addresses, request times, and error records, to operate and protect the services. If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Reclaim does not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or third-party product-analytics SDKs.

How information is used

  • Authenticate your account and authorize optional connections.
  • Verify activity, calculate rewards locally, and avoid duplicate credits.
  • Deliver earning configuration and maintain service reliability and security.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

Reclaim does not sell or rent personal information, serve advertising, or use your Health or Screen Time activity for marketing. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Your choices

  • Connected sources: open Settings → Sources & rates, choose Lichess, Strava, or Todoist, and disconnect. This stops new syncs and removes Reclaim’s connection credentials when the disconnect completes. Existing local reward receipts and previously earned time remain.
  • Apple Health: manage Reclaim’s read access in Apple Health → your profile → Apps → Reclaim. The exact labels may vary by iOS version.
  • Screen Time and notifications: manage permission in iOS Settings. Turning off Screen Time access prevents Reclaim from enforcing protection.
  • Sign out: signing out does not erase local history or automatically stop Screen Time enforcement.
  • Privacy requests: email us for access, correction, deletion, or other requests available under the laws that apply to you. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. Local history is not available to us through a cloud wallet.

Retention & account deletion

Local history remains on your device until it is cleared through account deletion or removed by app/device changes, subject to iOS backup and storage behavior. Reclaim does not provide a cloud wallet or streak restore service.

Account and connection records are kept while needed to maintain your account or an active connection. OAuth sessions expire after ten minutes. Strava webhook and revocation-job records have a seven-day expiry; processing can remove them sooner, and background expiry cleanup can occur later. Operational logs and support correspondence may be retained as needed for support, security, legal obligations, or under the service provider’s retention policies.

Older versions may have stored wallet recovery records and daily/weekly summaries in Firebase. The current app no longer uploads or restores those records. Existing legacy records may remain until removed through account deletion or cleanup.

Delete your account: open Reclaim Settings → Delete account and confirm with Apple. The deletion flow disconnects connected sources, removes known legacy cloud records and the Firebase Authentication account, revokes Apple authorization, and clears local history, protected-app selections, and Reclaim notifications. If the flow reports an error or you cannot sign in, contact us so we can help complete deletion and check for remaining server records.

Removing the app is different from deleting your account. Uninstalling does not delete your Firebase account or automatically disconnect credentials stored on Reclaim’s server. Deleting your Reclaim account does not delete your separate Apple, Lichess, Strava, or Todoist account.

Service providers & safeguards

Reclaim uses Google Firebase for authentication, configuration, and integration processing/storage; Apple services for sign-in, Health, Screen Time, and distribution; and, if connected, Lichess, Strava, and Todoist. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Support email is handled through the email providers involved. This website has no added analytics or advertising scripts and serves its fonts locally.

Reclaim limits what it sends off-device, uses authenticated service access, keeps Lichess credentials in Keychain, and encrypts server-held Strava and Todoist tokens. These measures reduce exposure but cannot guarantee that every system or transmission is risk-free.

Service providers may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate. Their own privacy policies also apply to data they receive independently when you use their services.
